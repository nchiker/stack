Join me on Stack

Pick your device. It takes a few minutes.

Phone Computer

On your phone

  1. Install Stack

    Tap the button. Android will ask once to allow installs from your browser.

    Install Stack

  2. Open Stack

    Tap I'm joining my partner.

  3. Meet your partner

    Scan their code on their phone.

On your computer

For Linux (Ubuntu, Debian, Mint).

  1. Install Stack

    Tap the button to download the file.

    Install Stack

  2. Open the file

    Double-click the file, then Install. Stack updates itself with your other updates.

  3. Connect your phone

    Open Stack and click Connect phone. On your phone, open Settings, then Sync, and scan the code.
